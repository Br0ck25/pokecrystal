Pokémon Crystal — Browser Preview

Explore the source-derived New Bark Town map and Professor Elm’s Laboratory. Use arrow keys or WASD to move between connected maps. On a phone, use the directional pad plus A to interact/confirm and B to cancel/close dialogue. This is an early browser recreation, not the complete game.
